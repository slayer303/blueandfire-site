Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

BLUE and FIRE Uploader ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the BLUE & FIRE TikTok account.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): the display name and avatar of the signed-in account, shown so the owner can check the right account is connected.
  • Video upload (video.upload): to send the owner's own video files to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.

What is stored

Only the TikTok access token, stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other people

The app does not collect any data about other TikTok users or viewers.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps and services) and delete the local token file.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/slayer303/blueandfire-site/issues.
